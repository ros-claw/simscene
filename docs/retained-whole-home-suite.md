# Aggregating retained original examinations

This separate delivery orchestrator is explicit memoization of complete original
evaluations. It does **not** claim to rerun `whole_home_suite_v7.verify_suite`, and
its own generic freeze may be later than a physical job. The original pre-job
f7, clock4, case, static, control and timing authorities remain unchanged.

Every one of eight distinct runs still requires its complete original f7
evaluation and an already retained independent receipt. All five positives also
require complete original clock4 certificates. The aggregation revalidates the
complete input/output byte closure, software, assets, runtime MDL dependencies,
case/contracts/reviews and independent custody. Controls additionally require a
reference binding recorded before their original evaluation; missing reference
proof cannot be added after the fact.

All original suite7 final comparison, chronology, inventory, runtime, editable
export, native-capture and CSV gates call the original frozen functions. A source
AST regression checks that the original final-gate block is unchanged. Complete
original reports are copied without reduction and their SHA values must remain
identical. No original raw trajectory is sliced, altered or replaced.

The original stock CSV ends at its narrower receiver box S and remains an
auxiliary artifact. The physical clock ends at the certified collapsed earliest
physical entry E. Because the supplied clock rejects extra rows, a new companion
CSV selects the **byte-identical header and prefix rows** for [release,E). The
full original CSV is preserved. The original clock and original clock4 prefix
function must agree on all four metrics; no booleans or missing steps are added.

The output labels its execution mode as `retained_original_report_aggregation`.
It accepts the designed physical suite only. Final films, actual visual review,
editable-project usability and publication remain separate decisions.
