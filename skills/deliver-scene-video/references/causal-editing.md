# Show the cause before the consequence

Use the existing continuous source as the physical reference. A short public
cut may omit travel and waits with clear cuts; label it an original-speed
excerpt and retain its source-frame map. Film duration is not useful-action time.
Keep the next receiver visible before a fast handoff and hold the terminal long
enough to see the result. Use a wide shot to establish distance, then a detail
view for latch/support/contact changes. Avoid cutting exactly on a collision
when that would hide whether the mechanism worked.

For fast dominoes first measure onset/tilt intervals from the existing report.
An ideal gravity-driven object's mass cancels against its inertia when shape
and mass ratios are unchanged. Spacing, aspect ratio, contact, friction and
damping can matter. Do not silently edit the accepted physical configuration
to make a promotional film feel slower. Use labelled slow replay, or make a
separate physical pacing variant and run its own controls.

Existing cameras can be edited without physics. If they do not resolve an
important action, `simscene replay prepare` extracts **exact recorded poses**
and `simscene replay render` renders another camera on a stopped timeline.
The latter is a recorded-pose re-render, not an original native camera or an
additional physical validation. Preserve source result/spec/lock hashes,
camera parameters, source steps, playback time and actual render receipt.
Missing poses fail; do not fill gaps or use AI motion interpolation.

At60Hz observed poses, source stride2 played at30fps is1×; stride1 at30fps is
0.5× with30 distinct observed poses per output second. Label speed and replay
provenance on screen. A re-rendered view must not be relabelled as a camera that
recorded the original task live. Keep original native source recordings.

Preview a few expensive new views first; reuse the extracted poses when only
the camera changes. Inspect decoded moving frames for stale imagery,
occlusion, highlights, visible transfer, aliasing and temporal ghosting. A
successful encoder is insufficient. Prefer a stable terminal view with both
support and receiver visible over a late camera chase.

For a split-screen intervention comparison, label two independent resets
throughout and align each side to its own upstream event. Do not imply shared
physical time or borrow frames from the main run. Static ending frames are
explicitly labelled; clipping a longer run does not create missing captures.

Sound is either recorded or identified as post-production design. Align a cup
click to the first physical contact callback, not full containment. Preserve
quiet before that beat; decode-check levels and sync. If no human audition or
phone playback happened, say so in the review instead of claiming it.

Save an actual edit manifest and frame map, plus a compact review record of
the frames and moving intervals inspected. A statement of style is not a
viewer comprehension test; report those separately if real viewers were used.
